# Clips Office

Status: approved

Clips Office creates safe internal clipping work: demo streamers, watch cycles, clip candidates, scores, clip packages, CapCut briefs, captions, posting drafts, artifacts, logs, and Human Gate requests.

It does not publish, upload, log into accounts, connect social platforms, spend money, or use unapproved real streamer content externally.
