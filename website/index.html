How it works

A clear path from source to decision.

  1. 01

    Bring in approved content

    Choose the source material or approved creator stream that the team is allowed to review.

  2. 02

    Detect and verify the moment

    Clipping Office examines timing, speech, audio, and frames so candidates are grounded in the media.

  3. 03

    Build the content package

    Prepare the vertical clip, transcript-aware caption, platform notes, and the files an editor needs.

  4. 04

    Review before publishing

    A human operator checks the final output, account, privacy choices, and destination before any external posting action.

Platform connections

Built around user choice and platform rules.

When a platform connection is enabled, Argentum requests only the permissions needed for the selected workflow. Users authorize their own account, can review the intended action, and can disconnect access.

  • Account authorization happens through the platform’s official consent flow.
  • Credentials and access tokens are never placed in public browser code.
  • Posting and other consequential account actions remain under Human Gate review.

Human Gate

Automation with a deliberate stop button.

Argentum can research, organize, draft, and prepare. High-impact actions—publishing, contacting customers, changing accounts, spending money, or changing credentials—require a human decision.

Common questions

What users should know.

Is Argentum a web app or desktop app?

The production workspace is a downloaded desktop app. This website explains the product, platform connections, support, and legal policies. Private operator tools remain behind authentication.

Does Argentum publish automatically?

No. Argentum prepares drafts and content packages. Consequential external actions are designed to stop for operator review and approval.

What does Clipping Office do?

Clipping Office helps identify promising moments, inspect transcripts and frames, prepare vertical edits, draft captions, and organize the final review package.

How can I request data deletion?

Visit the Support page for the current request process. Requests are verified before account or connected-platform data is deleted.
